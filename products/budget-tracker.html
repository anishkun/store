About

Find out where your money goes in five minutes a week. Log transactions with drop-down menus and the dashboard, charts and planners update themselves.

What's inside

  • Monthly Dashboard: pick a month to see income, expenses, net savings, savings rate and budget vs actual for every category, with charts.
  • Transactions: drop-down menus for type, category and payment method, with 1,500 rows ready to go.
  • Annual Summary: all 12 months side by side, with an income vs expenses trend chart.
  • Savings Goals: set a target and a date, and see how much to save each month.
  • Debt Payoff Planner: enter balance, interest rate and payment to see months to payoff, total interest and your debt-free date.
  • Settings: rename up to 25 expense categories and set budgets, starting balance and currency.
