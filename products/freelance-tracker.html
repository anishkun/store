About

Built for freelancers, consultants and small studios. Log invoices and expenses once, and the dashboard keeps track of everything else.

What's inside

  • Business Dashboard: invoiced, received, unpaid, overdue, expenses and profit for the year, with a month-by-month table and charts.
  • Invoices: due dates and status (Paid, Due, OVERDUE) are calculated for you, including days late.
  • Clients: total billed, paid and outstanding for each client.
  • Expenses: log business costs by category to see your real profit.
  • Tax set-aside: set your percentage and see how much to reserve and what you take home.
  • Settings: currency, payment terms, tax percentage and your own expense categories.

Why you'll like it

  • Works in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice and Apple Numbers.
  • No macros, no subscription, no account. Your client data stays with you.
  • Comes with sample data so you can see how it works, then clear it and start.
  • 1,000 invoice rows and 1,000 expense rows ready to use.
