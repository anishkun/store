About

Six tested n8n workflows that handle the jobs small businesses lose hours to every week: sorting email, following up on leads, typing invoices into spreadsheets, writing social posts and keeping up with industry news. Import a file, connect your accounts, switch it on.

What's inside

  • AI Email Triage + Draft Replies: sorts each new Gmail message into Lead, Support, Billing, Promo or Other, labels it, and writes a draft reply for leads and support. Nothing is sent without you.
  • AI Lead Qualifier: a hosted lead form. AI scores each lead 1 to 10, logs it to Google Sheets, sends an instant personal reply, and alerts you about hot leads.
  • AI Website Chatbot: a chat widget that answers from your FAQ, remembers the conversation, and emails you when a visitor asks for a person.
  • AI Invoice & Receipt Extractor: reads PDF invoices that arrive by email and writes vendor, number, dates, tax and total into Google Sheets.
  • AI Content Repurposer: paste a blog link and get a LinkedIn post, an X thread, an Instagram caption and a newsletter blurb, saved to a content sheet.
  • Daily AI News Digest: every morning it reads your RSS feeds and emails you a short briefing with links.

Why you'll like it

  • Sticky notes inside every workflow explain each step and what to change.
  • Uses OpenAI by default; swap in Claude, Gemini or a local model by changing one node.
  • Step-by-step setup guide: credentials, Google Sheets columns, testing and going live.
