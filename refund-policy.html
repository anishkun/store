When you can get a refund

Within 14 days of purchase, you can request a full refund if:

How to request a refund

Email anish03anish@gmail.com with your order ID and the reason. Approved refunds are issued through Dodo Payments to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.
