Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Summary

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Your personal data is only collected when you buy something or email me.

What is collected

When you buy a product, Dodo Payments (our merchant of record) collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details to process the order. Card and bank details are handled by Dodo Payments and its payment partners; I never see or store them. I receive your name, email, country and what you bought.

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

How it's used

Only to deliver your purchase, provide support and refunds, send important updates about a product you bought, and meet legal and tax record-keeping duties. Your data is never sold or used for advertising.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data at any time by emailing anish03anish@gmail.com. Some order records must be kept for tax purposes.
